Model the following problem as an RDDL domain, non-fluents block and instance.

Two gunslingers face each other. Each one is described by how much life they
have left and whether their weapon is currently loaded. On every time step the
agent controls one of them and may either reload, take a shot at the opponent,
or take cover.

Shooting only does damage if the weapon is loaded, and it empties the weapon.
A shot hits with a probability that depends on whether the target has taken
cover; a hit removes one point of life. Taking cover protects for the current
step only. Reloading makes the weapon loaded again.

The opponent acts on its own: whenever it is loaded it shoots, and otherwise it
reloads. The episode ends as soon as one of the two runs out of life. The agent
is rewarded for the opponent losing life and penalised for losing its own.

Use these names, because the evaluation depends on them:

  - state-fluent  agent-life       : integer, life left for the agent
  - state-fluent  opponent-life    : integer, life left for the opponent
  - state-fluent  agent-loaded     : boolean
  - state-fluent  opponent-loaded  : boolean
  - action-fluent shoot            : boolean
  - action-fluent reload           : boolean
  - action-fluent cover            : boolean

The instance must start both sides with the same life, an unloaded weapon on
both sides, at most one action per step, and a horizon of 100 steps.
